About

I am an undergraduate at the University of Michigan studying Computer Science (Robotics & Vision track) and Philosophy, with a 4.0/4.0 GPA. I work with Prof. Maani Ghaffari and Prof. Stella Yu at UMich, and previously with Prof. Mengdi Wang at Princeton.

My research interests lie in Embodied AI and Multimodal Robot Learning, focusing on developing scalable, predictive World Models that capture stable physical dynamics through robust latent state estimation. I aim to bridge multimodal perception and embodied spatial understanding to create actionable representations for reliable robotic decision-making and physical interaction.

LaWM

LaWM: Least Action World Models for Long-Horizon Physical Consistency from Visual Observations

Q. Xiao†, M. Ghaffari

CVPR 2027 · SubmittingWorld Model

[Paper] [Project Page] [GitHub]

Research details

Maani Ghaffari's Group, University of Michigan · Oct 2025 — Present

  • Proposed LaWM, a latent world-modeling framework that operationalizes the Principle of Least Action in learned visual latent space, making physical structure part of the rollout mechanism rather than a post-hoc regularizer.
  • Designed a latent variational integrator that encodes observations into generalized latent coordinates, learns a latent discrete Lagrangian, constructs a discrete action functional, and advances prediction through the discrete Euler–Lagrange condition.
  • Built an end-to-end visual prediction system with learned encoder, decoder, latent Lagrangian, context encoder, and differentiable finite-step DEL solver, enabling long-horizon rollout directly from visual observations.
  • Demonstrated improved physical invariance, background consistency, motion smoothness, and embodied prediction quality across physics-clean dynamics and RoboScape-style robot interaction benchmarks.
MACD

MACD: Model-Aware Contrastive Decoding via Counterfactual Data for Video-LLMs

Q. Xiao†, K. Zhou

ICML 2026Video-LLMHallucination

[Paper]

Research details

Lead-authored research · First & corresponding author

  • Proposed MACD, a training-free inference strategy that reduces Video-LLM hallucination by integrating model-aware counterfactual inputs into contrastive decoding for evidence-grounded token selection.
  • Built an object-/frame-level counterfactual pipeline: detect and track objects, initialize soft spatio-temporal masks, then optimize mask strengths via gradient ascent on the VLM loss to mine evidence-critical regions.

NeurIPS 2026ICLR 2026 WorkshopLLM Agents

[Paper] [GitHub]

Research details

Mengdi Wang's Group, Princeton University · May 2025 — Oct 2025

  • Developed GenEnv, a co-evolutionary framework where an LLM-based simulator generates difficulty-aligned curricula, shifting training from static supervision to a data-evolving paradigm.
  • Designed the α-Curriculum Reward to align task difficulty with the agent's Zone of Proximal Development, forming a co-evolution loop where the environment evolves to consistently challenge the agent.

LongNav-R1: Horizon-Adaptive Multi-Turn RL for Long-Horizon VLA Navigation

Y. Hu, A. Xi, Q. Xiao, …, R. Vasudevan†, M. Ghaffari†

RSS 2026VLARL

[Paper]

Research details

Maani Ghaffari's Group, University of Michigan · Oct 2025 — Present

  • Proposed LongNav-R1, an end-to-end multi-turn RL framework that reformulates long-horizon 3D embodied VLA navigation as a continuous conversation between policy and environment, with an implicit latent state in context.
  • Developed a horizon-adaptive mechanism (HAPO) with critic-free kernel regression and temporal-aware kernels for long-horizon credit assignment beyond single-turn BC.
